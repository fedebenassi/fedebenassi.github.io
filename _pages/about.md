Welcome to my academic website. I am a PhD student in Oceanography at the University of Bologna and CMCC, with research interests in marine and coastal systems, ocean dynamics, and the use of observational and numerical data to understand environmental change.

Research interests
======
- Physical oceanography and coastal processes
- Climate variability and marine systems
- Data analysis and numerical modelling

Current work
======
My work focuses on understanding how ocean processes evolve over time and how they can be described through integrated observational and modelling approaches.
